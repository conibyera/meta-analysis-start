Basic Meta-Analysis App

A free, web-based tool for running pairwise meta-analyses — forest plots, funnel plots, and meta-regression, without writing any code.

What it does

Upload a study-level dataset as a CSV or Excel file, and the app runs a complete pairwise meta-analysis from it. It accepts either pre-calculated effect sizes and standard errors, or raw counts (events and totals per group, including person-time for rate measures), so you don't need to compute effect sizes yourself before uploading.

Key features

  • Flexible input formats — works from summary effect sizes (with standard errors) or from raw event/total counts by treatment and control group.
  • Fixed effect, random effects, or both — compare pooling methods side by side.
  • Choice of between-study variance estimators — including Restricted Maximum-Likelihood — and confidence-interval methods for the random-effects estimate.
  • Forest plots — overall and broken out by subgroup.
  • Funnel plots and a formal test for asymmetry — to assess publication bias (the symmetry test requires at least 10 studies).
  • Meta-regression — explore how a single covariate relates to the pooled effect.
  • Multiple effect measures — including risk ratio, with support for other common binary, continuous, and rate-based measures.

Who it's for

Systematic reviewers, epidemiologists, and clinical researchers who need a standard pairwise meta-analysis — forest plot, heterogeneity, publication-bias check — without writing R or Stata code themselves.
